# VanLevel Support

VanLevel gives clear, practical caravan-levelling guidance using either your iPhone or the optional dedicated VanLevel Bluetooth sensor.

## Get started with this iPhone

1. On first launch, choose **This iPhone**, enter your caravan's actual measurements and confirm them.
2. Place the iPhone flat and screen up in portrait, with its top pointing to the caravan's front.
3. On a verified level surface, select **Set Current Position as Level**.
4. Keep the phone still while VanLevel settles, then follow the guidance.

Use the same phone orientation each time. Remove cases that rock on the camera bump, and avoid soft or curved surfaces.

## Get started with the VanLevel sensor

Choose **VanLevel sensor** during first-run setup, then open **Settings → Caravan & sensor setup**. Enter your actual measurements, choose the physical mounting orientation and save the confirmed setup to the sensor before relying on guidance.

Left and right mean the direction seen while inside the caravan facing the front / jockey-wheel end. Each installation preset names the USB-C port direction and screen direction. Recalibrate after changing a sensor orientation.

## Safety

Use suitable wheel chocks and supports, and follow the instructions for your caravan, jockey wheel, jack and levelling equipment. VanLevel provides measurement guidance; it does not replace safe parking, stabilisation or manufacturer requirements.
