# VanLevel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

VanLevel does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.

## Information used on your device

When you choose “This iPhone” as the measurement source, VanLevel uses the device motion sensors to calculate caravan pitch and roll. When you choose the optional VanLevel sensor, the app uses Bluetooth to receive levelling readings. These readings are processed on the device and are not sent to the developer or any third party.

Caravan dimensions, calibration values, preferences and saved sensor identifiers are stored locally on your device using Apple system storage.

## Diagnostic reports

VanLevel can create a diagnostic CSV report at your request. Reports remain on your device unless you explicitly choose to share one using the iOS share sheet. The destination you select is governed by that destination’s privacy practices.

## Accounts, analytics and advertising

VanLevel does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking technologies.

## Data retention and deletion

Local settings remain on your device until you change them or remove the app. Diagnostic reports can be deleted through the Files app or by removing the app and its data.

## Children’s privacy

VanLevel is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be published on the VanLevel support site.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the support link on the VanLevel App Store listing.
